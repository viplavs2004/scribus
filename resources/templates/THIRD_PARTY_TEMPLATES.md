# Third-party templates

Templates in this directory that did not originate in the Scribus project itself.

## Riku Leino templates (public domain)

The following templates are from the `scribus-template` 1.2.4.1 package (https://deb.debian.org/debian/pool/main/s/scribus-template/scribus-template_1.2.4.1.orig.tar.gz).
Their author, Riku Leino (c) 2004 <tsoots@gmail.com>, placed them into the public domain
(see https://metadata.ftp-master.debian.org/changelogs/main/s/scribus-template/stable_copyright). No attribution is required; it is given here as a courtesy.

They were converted from the Scribus 1.2 file format to the current format, fonts were mapped to
Arial / Times New Roman, and previews were regenerated. Fetched 2026-10-24.
